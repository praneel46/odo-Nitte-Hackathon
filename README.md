## About

DAYFLOW is a full-stack Human Resource Management System designed to simplify everyday HR operations for employees, HR teams and administrators.

It brings essential HR workflows into one centralized platform with a clean and professional user experience.

### What DAYFLOW provides

- Employee management
- Attendance tracking
- Check-in and check-out
- Break management
- Workday timeline
- Leave management
- Leave approval
- Payroll management
- Salary slips
- Document management
- Notifications
- Workforce analytics
- Audit logs
- Role-based access control
